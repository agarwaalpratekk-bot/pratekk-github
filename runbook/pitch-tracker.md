  allocated deals): for each row, update **Status** + **Last update** from the analyst's latest
  reply/founder-facing action; closure = in diligence or a clean pass.

  movement), send a **sharp, one- to three-line** reminder on their original allocation thread,
  To the analyst, cc Pratekk. No long emails. Stand down on a deal once it's closed; don't
  re-nudge the same person more than once a day, and don't nudge anyone on leave/holiday.
- Surface the open rows in the daily brief under **⏳ Delegated & gone quiet** with a
  days-pending clock, priority deals first (founders actively waiting).
