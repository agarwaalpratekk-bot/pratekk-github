2. **Jio Platforms closes today after a strong day 2.**
    - Retail was fully covered within two hours of opening on Thu (it was 0.73x on day 1).
    - The overall book was ~3.5x by 11:00 and roughly 5x later in the day. QIBs were bid ~9–12x.
    - Listing is around **28 Oct**.

    *Why it matters:* retail came back for Jio but not for Zetwerk. Brand and scale still pull retail money. For a seed fund this is mostly about liquidity, not a direct comparable.

    *Why it matters:* IvyCap is an active early-stage peer writing larger cheques with structured debt alongside equity. It's a useful reference for Fund II's follow-on reserve story.
