## Top 3 things to know

1. **Zetwerk's ₹5,012 Cr IPO opens today (15–19 Oct).** The price band is ₹143–151, which puts post-issue market cap at about ₹25,583 Cr (~$2.6–2.8B) at the top end. The issue is a ₹2,830 Cr fresh issue plus a ₹2,182 Cr offer for sale, and ₹2,160 Cr of the fresh money goes to repaying debt. Anchor bidding was yesterday, allotment is around 21 Oct and listing around 23 Oct. *Why it matters:* this is the biggest Indian manufacturing and deeptech-adjacent exit of the year, backed by Peak XV and Accel. How it lists will shape exit expectations for industrial and deeptech holdings like Integra Robotics and LogiXair. The anchor book is worth a line in LP conversations.
2. **Today was the original UPI MDR start date, and the fee does NOT start.** NPCI's 13 Oct deferral to "immediately post Diwali" still stands, and I found no new date. *Why it matters:* **the quarterly LP report is due today**, so check that the Mylapay and Spense lines say "deferred, date to be notified" rather than "live 15 Oct".
3. **11 rounds on Wed (StartupTalky), led by GreenCell Mobility (₹330 Cr, e-buses), with AI-led specialty chemicals again in focus: Scimplify raised about $25M led by Hitachi Ventures, two days after NuSynth's ₹36.5 Cr seed.** Separately, **Google.org committed $15M to The/Nudge to build AI tools for MSMEs** covering credit, compliance and market discovery. *Why it matters:* the AI-for-chemicals theme is drawing both corporate VCs (Hitachi) and climate funds. The Google.org grant overlaps with **Navanc's** MSME credit-underwriting space, so it may be a channel or partner rather than a competitor. Worth asking Navanc whether they're in touch with The/Nudge.
