A soft day 1 at Zetwerk, with Jio about to pull liquidity, is worth watching before you quote either in Fund II conversations.

The Shamrock round is unusually large for Endiya, whose first cheques usually run up to ~$2M, so treat it as unconfirmed.

3. **This week's count is now well over 25 rounds,** including Boldfit ₹400 Cr, Tessolve $75M, GreenCell ₹330 Cr, Scimplify ~$25M and Confido $12.5M. Weekly totals come out tomorrow, Sat 17 Oct. **There has still been no Indian fintech round this week.** *Why it matters:* money is going to consumer, deeptech and AI; fintech seed is quiet. That helps GrowthCap's pricing on fintech deals, but makes follow-ons harder for Mylapay and Spense while the UPI MDR date stays open.
