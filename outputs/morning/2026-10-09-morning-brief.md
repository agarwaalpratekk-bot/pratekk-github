## Top 3 things to know

1. **GST Council (57th meeting, Thu 8 Oct) scrapped GST officers' arrest powers** and raised the prosecution threshold from ₹1 Cr to ₹5 Cr. The general penalty drops from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000. *Why it matters:* lower criminal-exposure risk for founders across the portfolio. A good one-liner for the next LP update or a LinkedIn post.
2. **MoneyTruVa (Mumbai, financial-data infrastructure) raised a seed round led by Sunil Singhania (personal capacity)** with 20+ angels incl. Raj Shamani, Anant Ladha and Amar Ambani. Amount undisclosed; platform launch event 22 Oct in Mumbai. *Why it matters:* a Mumbai fintech-infra seed in your lane that went to a celebrity-angel syndicate, not a fund. Worth a look before the Series A, and a sign of where Mumbai fintech founders are raising seed.
3. **UPI MDR goes live in 6 days (Thu 15 Oct).** *Why it matters:* revenue tailwind for **Mylapay** (UPI acquiring) and relevant to **Spense** (credit on UPI). Good moment to ask both for a one-line view on Q3 impact.
