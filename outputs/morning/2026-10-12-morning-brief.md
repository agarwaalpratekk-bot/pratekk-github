## Top 3 things to know

1. **RBI raised the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.50% (7 Oct) and moved its stance from neutral to "calibrated tightening."** Secondary sources call it the first hike since Feb 2023; it was missed in last week's briefs. *Why it matters:* debt gets dearer for lending-heavy portfolio companies (Navanc, Spense's bank partners, NeoGrowth-type comparables), and venture-debt terms will tighten. Worth a line in the 15 Oct LP report.
2. **Weekly funding confirmed low: Inc42 counts $122.9M across 25 deals (5–9 Oct), down 47.4% WoW; Entrackr counts $113.35M across 28 startups (5–10 Oct).** This replaces Saturday's $168M YourStory figure, which covered a different window (3–9 Oct, including myTVS). *Why it matters:* the slowest week in over a month, but early stage held up (Entrackr: 22 of 28 deals were early stage, $58M). Seed valuations should stay disciplined.
3. **UPI MDR goes live Thu 15 Oct (3 days).** Reported as 0.4% on select P2M transactions above ₹2,000 (secondary source; confirm on NPCI). *Why it matters:* a direct revenue lever for **Mylapay**. **Spense**'s Credit Line on UPI (Unity SFB × Paytm) was tentatively due for wider release today, per its Sept update (internal).
