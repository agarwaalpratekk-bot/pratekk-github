## Top 3 things to know

1. **The Tonbo Imaging IPO closed ~136x subscribed** (Business Standard, final day). QIBs bid 293.6x, NIIs 170.9x and retail 31.1x for the ₹852 Cr all-OFS issue at ₹665–700. Allotment is Wed 21 and listing Fri 23 Oct.

    *Why it matters:* this is the clearest signal yet that public markets want Indian defence deeptech. It's a live comparable for the LogiXair and Integra exit story in Fund II conversations. **Zetwerk's final number isn't out yet.** It was only ~0.3x on day 2, despite a ₹2,251 Cr anchor book, so treat the two as opposite reads until Zetwerk's close is published.

2. **Even Healthcare raised a $50M Series B at a ~$300M valuation**, led by existing investor Khosla Ventures with Alpha Wave (TechCrunch, 19 Oct). That's triple its valuation from 18 months ago. Also on Monday: **The Pant Project raised ₹109 Cr (Series B)**, led by Mirae Asset, with venture debt from Alteria.

    *Why it matters:* Even's existing lead tripled down at a higher price. Insiders leading follow-ons is the signal GrowthCap will want from co-investors when its portfolio raises next.

3. **Jio Platforms opens tomorrow (Wed 21 Oct)**, alongside Paras Healthcare.

    *Why it matters:* a mega-issue can soak up retail and HNI liquidity the same week Tonbo and Zetwerk list. Expect some noise in new-age listings.
