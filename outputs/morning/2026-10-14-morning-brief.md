## Top 3 things to know

1. **NPCI deferred UPI MDR (yesterday, 13 Oct). It will NOT go live on Thu 15 Oct.** NPCI's steering committee pushed the 0.4% fee on select P2M transactions above ₹2,000 to "immediately post Diwali, at a date to be notified." Reports say the fee structure is unchanged, and some point to 1 Jan 2027. CAIT welcomed the deferral, and opposition parties had called the fee a tax on consumers. *Why it matters:* **Mylapay's** MDR revenue tailwind slips by at least a quarter. Correct any LP-report line that says "live 15 Oct". **Don't post Monday's LinkedIn Draft B** ("UPI starts paying for itself from Thursday"); it's now wrong.
2. **Two deeptech seeds with climate and industrial investors: NuSynth Technologies raised ₹36.5 Cr (~$4M) seed co-led by Avaana Capital and Shastra VC (AI-led chemical R&D), and ThinkMetal raised ₹27 Cr led by IAN Alpha Fund with 3i Partners.** *Why it matters:* deeptech seed cheques of ₹25–40 Cr are becoming normal. Avaana, Shastra and IAN Alpha are worth knowing as co-investors for GrowthCap's deeptech pipeline.
3. **Funding stays strong this week: 7 rounds on Tue (StartupTalky), led by Mesa School of Business (₹110 Cr) and MakeO (₹90 Cr, Singularity AMC), on top of Monday's Boldfit ₹400 Cr.** *Why it matters:* the slowdown in the 5–10 Oct week looks like a one-week dip rather than a trend.
