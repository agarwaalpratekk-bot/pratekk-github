## Top 3 things to know

1. **Zetwerk's IPO closed at ~3.1x, not the "~0.3x" I quoted for day 2.**
    - The final figure is 3.06x according to Entrackr and 3.10x according to HDFC Sky.
    - QIBs bid 4.71x and NIIs 1.45x, but **retail came in at only 0.77x**.
    - Allotment is today and listing is Fri 23 Oct, the same day as Tonbo, which closed at ~136x.

    *Why it matters:* the two listings send different signals. Institutions bought both, but retail investors only wanted defence deeptech (Tonbo), not manufacturing platforms (Zetwerk). Wait for Friday's listing prices before using either as a comparable for Integra or LogiXair in Fund II conversations.

2. **Jio Platforms opens today (21–23 Oct) as India's largest-ever IPO.**
    - The issue is ~₹30,213 Cr, all fresh shares, priced at ₹1,065–1,119.
    - The anchor book was reportedly bid more than $20B. One digest puts the anchor allocation at ₹8,110 Cr, but I couldn't confirm that figure.
    - Listing is around 29 Oct.

    *Why it matters:* an issue this size will soak up institutional and HNI money for the rest of the week. Expect Friday's Tonbo and Zetwerk listings to trade amid that pull on liquidity.

3. **No new early-stage rounds yesterday because of the holiday.** Monday's deals (Even Healthcare $50M, The Pant Project ₹109 Cr, So/Facet ₹10 Cr) were covered in yesterday's brief.
