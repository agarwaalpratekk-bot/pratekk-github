# GrowthCap Morning Brief — Wed 7 Oct 2026

*Portfolio news, peer India VC funds, every deal in the weekly headline, market and regulation pulse. Public data only.*

## Top 3 things to know
1. **UPI MDR goes live in 8 days (15 Oct).** NPCI's final structure: 0.4% on P2M UPI payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 (for ₹75k+). Railways, telecom, insurance and fuel pay a flat ₹5. **Capital-market payments pay 0.02%.** The revenue is shared across issuer banks, beneficiary banks, payer PSPs, **merchant-acquiring payment aggregators** and NPCI. The ecosystem pool is up to ~₹16,000 Cr a year. *Why it matters:* this is a direct revenue line for **Mylapay** (UPI acquiring). Ask the founder how much of the cut flows to an acquiring platform like theirs.
2. **Cabinet cleared the ₹10,000 Cr SME Growth Fund (6 Oct, per StartupTalky).** It was first announced in Budget 2026 as equity capital for MSMEs. *Why it matters:* it is likely to run through AIFs as a fund-of-funds. Watch for the operating guidelines, since GrowthCap Fund II could be an applicant.
3. **PanIIT Alumni India and the Andhra Pradesh government are planning a ₹500 Cr deeptech fund** (5 Oct). *Why it matters:* it is a new competitor and potential co-investor for deeptech and defence deals like Integra and LogiXair.
