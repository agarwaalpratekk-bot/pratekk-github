    - The book was reportedly fully covered within about 100 minutes of opening.

    *Why it matters:* retail is lukewarm again (0.73x, like Zetwerk's 0.77x). Institutional money is carrying the IPO window. Friday's listings will show whether the Tonbo-style "defence deeptech premium" holds.

3. **The India–Mauritius tax treaty now has a Principal Purpose Test.** It was notified on 13 Oct (Notification 135/2026) and applies to transactions from 10 Aug 2026.
    - Treaty benefits can now be denied where getting them was one of the main purposes of an arrangement.

    *Why it matters:* for Fund II's international LP pitch, any Mauritius-routed LP structure now has to show real substance. Raise it with Kaytes and NDA before you finalise the Fund II PPM and the Europe/Luxembourg conversations.
