# GrowthCap Morning Brief — Mon 2 Nov 2026

*Public data only. You're at the IVCA Emerging Managers Roundtable in Delhi today.*

## Top 3 things to know

1. **Indian startups raised ~$115M across 28 deals in 26–31 Oct** (Entrackr).
    - That's down ~14% from $133.9M the week before, and well below the 8-week average of ~$210M across 23 deals.
    - Growth stage: $42.3M across 4 deals, led by Simplismart and Live2.ai ($14M each).
    - Early stage: 24 deals, led by Cent ($15M Series A, Blume).

    *Why it matters:* deal count nearly doubled (28, from 15), but cheque sizes are small. Seed and early stage is busy, and growth capital is thin. That's a useful talking point for today's room.

2. **Bengaluru took 14 of the 28 deals.** Delhi-NCR had 5 and Mumbai 3.

    *Why it matters:* Mumbai is under-represented, which is a sourcing gap and an opportunity for a Mumbai-based fund.

3. **No acquisitions or fund launches were reported this week.**
