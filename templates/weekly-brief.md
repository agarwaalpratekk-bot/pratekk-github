## Rules
- Funnel numbers are **floors**, labelled as such, until WhatsApp lands. Never present as
  the complete pipeline.
- Pull the week's **EOD reports** (Neeti/Shruti/Smridh) and **MoM** emails as the primary
  structured source.
- The investor draft must pass the **portfolio-attribution fact-check** before it's offered.
- Draft only — Pratekk sends.
