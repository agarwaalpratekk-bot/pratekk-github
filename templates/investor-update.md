# Template — Investor update engine (System 3)

Reverse-engineered from **"GCV Fund 1_Q1 FY 2027_Quaterly Investor Update"** (sent 3 Sep
2026 to an LP, cc Neeti + investor@growthcap.vc). Purpose: keep existing LPs and Fund II
prospects continuously warm in Pratekk's voice — the cheapest way to stay top-of-mind
without asking for anything.

## Cadences
- **Weekly / monthly touch** — short "note from the GP's desk" (see `templates/weekly-brief.md`).
- **Quarterly** — the full structured update below.

## Quarterly structure (observed)
1. **Cover note** — milestone-led opener ("This quarter marks an important milestone…"),
   then a bulleted "inside this update you'll find" list.
2. **Portfolio highlights** — recent milestones per company.
3. **The new investment** — spotlight (Q1 FY27: Integra Robotics, the 7th portfolio co).
4. **Sector outlook & investment thesis** — FinTech / AI / DeepTech.
5. **From the GP's desk** — Fund I status (final close done) + Fund II (₹1,000 Cr) progress.
6. **Team additions, brand-building, and key asks** from the LP community.
7. **Sign-off:** `Best Regards, / Pratekk Agarwaal / Founder & General Partner`.

## Voice (formal register — see docs/02-voice-profile.md)
Warm, confident, milestone-led. "we are pleased to share", "thank you for your continued
trust and partnership", "back ambitious founders building category-defining companies".
Never salesy; the ask is soft and specific.

## ⚠️ Required fact-check before any draft is offered
An LP (Venkatesan) caught **two portfolio companies mislabelled as the same sector** in the
real Q1 update. Before offering a draft, verify:
- [ ] Each portfolio company's **sector/business** is stated correctly and distinctly.
- [ ] Company **names** spelled right (TrusTerra, Navanc, Reput, Integra Robotics, …).
- [ ] Any **numbers** (portfolio count, fund size ₹1,000 Cr, close status) are current.
- [ ] Nothing **confidential** to one LP leaks into a broadcast update.

## Data sources
- Portfolio milestones: MoM emails, EOD reports, portfolio founder threads (+ WhatsApp later).
- Fund status: internal compliance/IR threads, Kaytes/Trustee correspondence.
- New investments: deal-close threads.

## Guardrail
Draft only. Pratekk reviews and sends. Bracket every field that needs his call on what is
shareable with which audience.
